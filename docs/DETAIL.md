A plush toy wearing DSH

![Muse Plush, light](preview/light.png)

## What it does

- **Pile everywhere**: the sidebar is bare fur, the conversation gets a translucent powder-blue wash over it, so text stays readable.
- **Fuzzy bubbles**: user bubbles and the composer are beige plush with edges roughened by an SVG displacement filter applied to the container only — text is untouched.
- **Brass buttons**: brand mark and send button become the buttons on the plush toy's belly.
- **Little Muse**: a small plush sits on the composer, blinks and bobs; can be switched off.
- **Night**: dark blue-grey pile with a dim beige belly.

![Muse Plush, dark](preview/dark.png)

## Settings

Settings → Skins → Muse Plush: mascot toggle, fuzzy edges toggle, pile length (short / normal / long), fur colour, ear/belly colour.

## Compatibility

DSH Web 0.2.2-rc series. Styles are scoped to `body[data-dsh-muse-plush]`; no Harness client packages are imported, and nothing is left behind on removal.
